---
title: Getting started with Ledgerly
category: Basics
---
Ledgerly is invoicing software for freelancers and small studios. You create clients, send invoices, take card or bank payments and track what you're owed.

## Create your account
Sign up with your email address and choose a password of at least 10 characters. We send a confirmation link that stays valid for 24 hours. Until you confirm, you can draft invoices but not send them.

## Set up your business details
Open Settings, then Business profile. Add your business name, address and tax number. These appear on every invoice, so fill them in before sending your first one. You can upload a logo in PNG or SVG format, up to 2 MB.

## Choose your currency
Your default currency is set from your country when you sign up. Change it under Settings, then Business profile, then Default currency. Each invoice can still use a different currency.
