## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication under Settings, then Security. Ledgerly supports authenticator apps. Save your 10 backup codes somewhere safe; each works once.

## Lost access to two-factor
If you lose your authenticator and backup codes, contact support from the email address on the account. For your security, recovery takes up to 3 business days and requires identity verification.

## Sessions
You can see every signed-in device under Settings, then Security, and sign out of any of them.
