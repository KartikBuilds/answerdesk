---
title: Refunds and credit notes
category: Payments
---
## Refund a card payment
Open the paid invoice and select Refund. You can refund the full amount or part of it, up to 180 days after the payment. Refunds take 5 to 10 business days to reach the client's card. The original processing fee isn't returned.

## Credit notes
A credit note reduces what a client owes without changing the original invoice. Use it to correct a paid invoice or to give a discount after sending. Credit notes have their own numbering, for example CN-0007.
