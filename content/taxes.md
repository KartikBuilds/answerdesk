---
title: Tax on invoices
category: Invoices
---
## Add a tax rate
Create tax rates under Settings, then Taxes, for example GST 18%. Apply one or more rates to each line item. Ledgerly shows each tax separately on the invoice.

## Tax-inclusive prices
If your prices already include tax, turn on Prices include tax. Ledgerly then works out the tax portion from the total instead of adding it on top.

## Tax reports
The Tax report under Reports shows tax collected per rate for any date range, and can be exported as CSV. Ledgerly doesn't file returns for you.
