## Invoice in another currency
Choose the currency when you create an invoice. Ledgerly supports 135 currencies.

## Payouts in another currency
Card payments in a currency other than your payout currency are converted at the payment partner's daily rate, with a 1% conversion fee.

## Reports
Reports show totals in your default currency, converted at the rate on the payment date.
