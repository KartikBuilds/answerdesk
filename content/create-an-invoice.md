---
title: Creating and sending an invoice
category: Invoices
---
## Create an invoice
Select New invoice, choose a client, then add line items with a description, quantity and unit price. Ledgerly calculates subtotals, tax and the total automatically.

## Invoice numbers
Invoices are numbered automatically in sequence, for example INV-0042. You can change the prefix under Settings, then Invoices. Numbers can't be reused once an invoice has been sent, because many tax authorities require an unbroken sequence.

## Send an invoice
Select Send to email the invoice to your client as a link and a PDF attachment. You can also copy the link and share it yourself. The client doesn't need a Ledgerly account to view or pay.

## Edit a sent invoice
You can edit an invoice until it's paid. The client sees the latest version through the same link, and the history shows every change. To change a paid invoice, issue a credit note instead.
