## Card payments
Clients can pay by Visa, Mastercard and American Express from the invoice link. Card payments are processed by our payment partner. The fee is 2.9% plus 0.30 USD per successful payment, deducted before payout.

## Bank transfer
You can add your bank details to invoices so clients can pay by transfer. Ledgerly doesn't process these payments, so mark the invoice as paid when the money arrives.

## Payouts
Card payments are paid out to your connected bank account 2 business days after the payment succeeds. The first payout after connecting a new bank account can take up to 7 days while it's verified.

## Partial payments
Turn on Allow partial payments on an invoice to let clients pay part of the total. The invoice stays open until the full amount is received.
