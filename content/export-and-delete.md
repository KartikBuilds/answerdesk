---
title: Exporting data and closing your account
category: Account
---
## Export your data
Under Settings, then Data, select Export. You'll receive a ZIP file by email within an hour, containing CSV files of clients, invoices and payments, plus PDFs of every invoice.

## Close your account
Only the account owner can close the account, under Settings, then Data, then Close account. Any active Pro subscription is cancelled at the same time. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently deleted. We keep invoice records longer only where the law requires it.
