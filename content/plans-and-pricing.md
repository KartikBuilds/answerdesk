## Free plan
The Free plan includes up to 5 active clients and 10 invoices per month. Card payments are available with the standard processing fee. Ledgerly branding appears in the invoice footer.

## Pro plan
Pro costs 12 USD per month, or 120 USD per year when billed annually. It includes unlimited clients and invoices, recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders, custom invoice branding without the Ledgerly footer, and team members.

## Team members
Pro includes 2 team members. Each additional member costs 4 USD per month.

## Changing plans
You can upgrade at any time and the new features unlock immediately. If you downgrade, you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Existing invoices are never deleted when you downgrade.
