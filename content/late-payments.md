---
title: Reminders and late payments
category: Payments
---
## Automatic reminders
On Pro, Ledgerly can email reminders before and after the due date. The default schedule is 3 days before, on the due date, and 7 days after. Change the schedule under Settings, then Reminders.

## Late fees
You can add a late fee as a fixed amount or a percentage of the unpaid total. It's added once, the day after the due date. Check local rules before charging late fees, because some places limit them.

## Overdue invoices
An invoice becomes overdue the day after its due date. Overdue invoices appear at the top of the dashboard in amber.
