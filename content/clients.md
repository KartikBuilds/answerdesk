## Add a client
Select Clients, then New client. A name and an email address are required. Add a billing address and tax number if you need them on invoices.

## Import clients
Import clients from a CSV file under Clients, then Import. The file needs name and email columns. Up to 1,000 clients can be imported at a time.

## Archive a client
Archived clients don't count toward the Free plan's 5-client limit. Their invoices stay in your records and reports.
