---
title: Team members and permissions
category: Account
---
Team members are available on the Pro plan.

## Invite a team member
Go to Settings, then Team, and enter their email address. The invitation is valid for 7 days.

## Roles
Owners can do everything, including billing and deleting the account. Admins can manage clients, invoices and settings but not billing. Members can create and send invoices but can't change settings or see reports.

## Remove a team member
Removing someone signs them out immediately. Invoices they created stay in the account.
