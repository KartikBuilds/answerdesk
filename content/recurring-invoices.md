---
title: Recurring invoices
category: Invoices
---
Recurring invoices are available on the Pro plan.

## Set up a recurring invoice
Create an invoice as usual, then turn on Repeat. Choose weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, the start date, and optionally an end date or a number of occurrences.

## Automatic sending
Each new invoice is created at 08:00 in your account's time zone on its scheduled day and sent to the client automatically. If you prefer to review first, choose Create as draft instead of Send automatically.

## Automatic card charges
If the client saved a card when paying a previous invoice and agreed to future charges, Ledgerly can charge that card on the due date. The client gets a receipt by email.
